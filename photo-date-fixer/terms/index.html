Using Photo Date Fixer

Photo Date Fixer is a free browser tool, operated by Peak Apps Studio, that writes dates, times and locations into copies of the photos and videos in your Snapchat My Data export. By using it, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the tool.

You need no account. The tool is meant for people who can use Snapchat under Snap's own terms.

What the tool does

You add the ZIP files of your export. The tool reads them in your browser, matches each photo and video to its entry in the memories list and writes fixed copies into new ZIP parts, together with a CSV report. We never receive your files. Everything happens on your device, as the privacy policy explains. Your original ZIP files are not changed.

What we ask of you

  • Keep your original export files, and do not delete them until you have checked the fixed copies.
  • Check the results. The CSV report shows how each file was matched and which entries had no file. Read it before you delete anything.
  • Use the tool with your own export, or with an export you have permission to use.
  • Mind what you share. The fixed photos and the report contain dates, times and locations.

No warranty

The tool is provided as it is, without promises about its results or its availability. Snapchat changes its export format from time to time, so some files may not match a memory, or may match only to within 2 seconds. We may change or stop the tool at any time.
