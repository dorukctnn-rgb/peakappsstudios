Guide · Wording

Quilt label wording: what to write, with examples

Short answer: at the least, your full name and when and where the quilt was made. Then, if there’s room: who it’s for and why, the quilt’s name, who quilted it, the pattern, one line of message and a washing note. Specific facts (“pieced from his flannel shirts”) outlast general sentiments.

The minimum and the longer list come from the Quilt Alliance, “Label it Already!” (19 October 2019): maker, date, location, purpose, recipient, dimensions, fiber content, techniques, pattern, title and the maker’s contact details. Last checked 8 October 2026.

Not every label needs every line. A small label with a name, a place and a year is still a good label; the label maker shrinks the text to fit and tells you when it gets too small to print well on fabric.

Washing notes

Write the note you would give the owner for the quilt as a whole: the fabrics, the batting and how hard it will be used. The Quilts of Valor Foundation’s suggestion for its cotton quilts is cold water, a mild detergent, a color catcher the first time, and tumble dry low.

If the label itself is printed on an inkjet fabric sheet, its maker’s care advice also applies. Electric Quilt recommends washing its printed sheets by hand, only when necessary, with a mild detergent, and Jacquard says labels printed on its sheets are not washfast. On a quilt that will go through the machine, a printed label may fade before the quilt does; compare the sheets before you choose.

Five things that make a label useless later

  1. Only “Mom” or “Grandma”. Keep the warmth and add the full name: “Grandma Jo (Josephine Miller)”.
  2. No year. “Christmas” or “her birthday” loses its meaning within a generation.
  3. No place. The town and state are what tie a quilt to a family history.
  4. Ink that fades. The Quilt Alliance recommends an archival, permanent, fadeproof pen for handwritten labels, not an office pen.
  5. Words too small to print. Below about 7 pt, inkjet text on fabric blurs. Fewer words or a bigger label fixes it.
