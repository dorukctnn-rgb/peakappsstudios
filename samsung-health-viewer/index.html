Get your Samsung Health data onto the computer

Samsung Health saves the download on the phone itself; nothing is emailed. These are the steps from Samsung’s support page (menu names can differ a little between app versions):

On the phone

  1. Open Samsung Health, tap the three dots (More options), then Settings.
  2. Find Download personal data, tap Download and allow what it asks. Sign in to your Samsung account if asked, wait until it finishes, and tap OK.
  3. Open My Files, then Downloads, then Samsung Health. Your data is the folder inside whose name starts with samsunghealth_.

To the computer

  1. In My Files, touch and hold that folder, tap More, then Compress. A ZIP appears next to it.
  2. Send the ZIP to yourself by email, Google Drive, OneDrive or Quick Share. Or connect the phone with a USB cable and copy the folder.
  3. On the computer, choose the ZIP or the folder at the top of this page. There is no need to unzip it.

Source: Samsung’s support page Download or erase your personal data from Samsung Health, last checked 22 October 2026. More detail in how to export your Samsung Health data.

Questions

Is there a Samsung Health app for PC?

No. Samsung Health is an app for Galaxy phones and watches, and Samsung has not released a Windows, Mac or web version. An Android emulator can run the phone app on a PC, but you then sign in to your health account on the emulator. Wristory reads the data the app lets you download instead.

Is my health data uploaded?

No. The page reads the files with your browser, on your computer. You can load the page, switch off the network and open your files: it keeps working. The only request Wristory makes is the Pro license check, which sends the key to Gumroad and nothing else. When you click a Pro button, a counter of our own adds one to a daily total for this tool, with the page and the kind of site that sent you: no cookies, no personal data, no IP address kept, and never your files.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free: the last 90 days in your export, with the day sheet and every metric the export holds. Pro, $7 once, opens every year and adds year against year, the Excel workbook and CSV files, the PDF report, the year poster and the full blood pressure and weight tables.

Can I open Samsung Health data in Excel?

The download is a folder of CSV files, one per kind of data, so Excel opens each one, but the columns are raw: times in UTC with a separate offset, durations in milliseconds, sleep stages as codes such as 40003. Wristory Pro writes one workbook with a row per day in plain units, plus sheets for workouts, sleep, weight and blood pressure.

My ZIP asks for a password. What now?

A download requested on Samsung’s privacy website comes as a ZIP locked with a password that Samsung emails to you. Browsers cannot open locked ZIPs. Unzip it on the computer with that password, then choose the unzipped folder here. The download from the app itself has no password.

Why are some days empty?

A day is empty when neither the phone nor the watch recorded anything, for example while the watch was charging. Wristory does not fill gaps or guess.

Can I show this to my doctor?

Pro makes a PDF report of the period you pick: plain averages, a week-by-week table and every blood pressure and weight reading. It is your own record from a consumer phone and watch, not a medical test, and Wristory does not interpret it.

Does it read downloads from older phones?

Yes. Downloads from older versions of the app (daily rows with a number instead of a date, files without the first line about the file, phones without a watch) are read too. If a file cannot be read, the list of what Wristory read says which one and why.

Wristory shows your own numbers as your phone and watch recorded them. It is not a medical device, it is not a diagnosis, and it gives no medical advice.
