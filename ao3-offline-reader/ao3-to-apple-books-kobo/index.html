Adding AO3 fics to Apple Books or a Kobo, step by step

Both Apple Books and Kobo take EPUB files you add yourself. Save the fic as a clean EPUB in Ficshelf, then follow the steps from Apple or Kobo for your device. Each step below comes from their own help pages, read on October 31, 2026.

Open your downloads in Ficshelf

The short answer

  1. Open the fic in Ficshelf and press Save as clean EPUB.
  2. Apple Books on a Mac: File > Import, or drag the file into the library.
  3. Kobo eReader: connect it by USB and drag the EPUB onto the KOBOeReader drive.

Apple Books

Adding an EPUB to Apple Books
DeviceSteps
MacApple says: go to the Books app, choose File > Import, select the books you want, then click Import. You can also drag and drop books into your library. Apple notes that many EPUB books can be imported, and that some other types (ACSM, AZW, ODM) open only in their own app.
iPhone and iPadSave the EPUB in the Files app. Apple's Pages guide says to tap an EPUB saved in Files to view it in the Books app. That guide is about Pages exports; we found no separate Apple page for other EPUB files, so try the tap and write to us if it does not open.

Kobo

Adding an EPUB to a Kobo eReader or the Kobo app
DeviceSteps from Kobo's help
Kobo eReader, with a computerTurn on the eReader. Connect it to the computer with the USB cable. Tap Connect when "Computer detected" appears. Find the drive named KOBOeReader and drag the PDF or EPUB files into it. Eject the drive, unplug the cable, and you will see "Importing content". Then Home > My Books.
Kobo Books app on iPhone or iPadKobo lists three ways for non-protected EPUB or PDF files: Google Drive (share the file and open it in Kobo Books), the Mail app (email the file to yourself, tap and hold it, Share, Kobo Books), or Finder on a Mac (drag the file over the Kobo Books icon).

Kobo adds that files added this way must not have DRM, and that they only appear on the device you imported them to, even after you sync. AO3 downloads are not DRM-protected files.

Make the EPUB first: open the fic in Ficshelf and press Save as clean EPUB. It happens in your browser.

Questions

How do I add AO3 fics to Apple Books?

On a Mac, open Books, choose File > Import and select the EPUB, or drag it into the library. On an iPhone, save the EPUB in Files and tap it, which Apple's Pages guide describes opening in Books.

How do I upload an AO3 fic to Kobo?

Connect the Kobo eReader to a computer by USB, tap Connect, and drag the EPUB onto the KOBOeReader drive. Eject it and the book appears under My Books.

Can I send an AO3 EPUB to the Kobo app on my iPhone?

Yes, by Kobo's own steps: share the file to Kobo Books from Google Drive or the Mail app, or drag it over the Kobo Books icon in Finder on a Mac.

Will my reading place sync?

Kobo says items added this way appear only on the device you imported them to. For Apple Books, Apple's page says iCloud for Books shows imported books on your other devices.
