Questions owners ask us.

Do I need a domain already?

No. We register one in your name and the first year is included. If you already have one, we connect it and your e-mail addresses keep working.

What if I don’t like the draft?

Then you pay nothing. Tell us what to change, or simply ignore the e-mail.

Who owns the site?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and if you ever move on we send you every file.

Can I change prices and hours later?

Yes. E-mail us the change and it is on the site within two working days; small updates like prices, hours and photos are part of the yearly fee. New pages or sections are priced separately, and you get the price before we start.

Which languages can the site have?

Up to three in either package, for example English, Turkish and Russian. We write the translations and check every page in each language before launch.

Will the 3D work on an old phone?

Every moving part has a still picture that loads first. Slow phones, and visitors who turn motion off, keep the still version, so the page stays quick and easy to read.

Can you build an online shop or a booking system?

We don’t build full online shops. We make booking and ordering simple through WhatsApp or e-mail, or link to the booking tool you already use.

Who are you?

Peak Apps Studio, a small studio in Antalya, Turkey. We also make apps, games and web tools, which you can see on our home page. We work by e-mail, in English and Turkish.
