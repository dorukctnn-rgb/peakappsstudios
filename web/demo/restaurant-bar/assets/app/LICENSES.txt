1. Scene code: pmndrs/examples "caustics" (MIT)
=====================================================================
The scene is adapted from the "caustics" example of pmndrs/examples
(https://github.com/pmndrs/examples/tree/main/examples/caustics, original by Paul Henschel).
Changes: warm bar light instead of the city HDRI, a liquid and a coaster with the brand name, a dark bar top,
the cake, flowers and fork removed, camera driven by page scroll and pointer, quality tiers for phones.

"Ikea - Pokal Glass Cups" by CDcruz
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/21837e54a14346aa900e1ae719779b86
  Part of the glass and straw model of the original example.

"Cute milkshake" by Felix Yadomi
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/3ba52a41b4b248df953684861d9e7a20
  Part of the glass and straw model of the original example.

Changes made: only the glass and the straw were kept (cake, flowers, fork, second straw and cameras were removed),
and the file was re-saved without Draco compression with glTF-Transform. Colour, material and lighting are
set by the scene code.
The cake slice (matousekfoto) and dried flower (Second Studio) models of the original example are NOT used.
