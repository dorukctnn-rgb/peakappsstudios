It started in a hardware store

I’m Kelly Ging, owner of Kelly Lou Cakes café and boutique bakery in Portlaoise, Co Laois. In our bakery every delicious treat is made from scratch with the finest ingredients and a lot of love!

My story began over 10 years ago when, as a passionate baker with a sweet tooth and a love for creating beautiful desserts, I decided to turn my passion into a business and begged my Dad for a small corner in his hardware store, where I set up with a small domestic oven and a coffee machine.
