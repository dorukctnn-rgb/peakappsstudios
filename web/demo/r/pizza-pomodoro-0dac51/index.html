How we make it

01 / 04

  1. The dough

    Homemade every day. It must be soft and pliable, with a moist centre.

  2. The toppings

    Fresh ingredients on a Neapolitan base: tomato, mozzarella, basil and E.V.O.O.

  3. The oven

    Into the wood-fired oven for 60 to 90 seconds at 400 to 450 degrees.

  4. The crust

    A cornicione that is light and fluffy. It should be folded when you eat it.
