Texan origins

A lively, welcoming family-run restaurant inspired by all things Texan. David and Lisa first met working in a restaurant on the Riverwalk in San Antonio, and Boo Koos brings back the things they missed most.

In 2014 they turned Shimmy's, their milkshake bar in Helston, into Boo Koos, to share the spiced, handmade burger recipe they had created in the States.

Five years later came our not-so-little baby brother in Falmouth, with 28 tables and more Tex-Mex and American barbecue. Still family owned, and still hands on.

David & Lisa
