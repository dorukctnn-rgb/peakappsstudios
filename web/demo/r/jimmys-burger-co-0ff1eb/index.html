Created in 2016

Jimmys was created in 2016 for the lovers of burgers, booze and good times.

Since we opened our doors in 2016, we have always had a clear mission to support our community.

Our values include taking care of our team, sourcing premium ingredients from local providers, and supporting our community through donations, events and sponsorships.

Find us on the corner of Shields Street and Sheridan Street in Cairns. We are open seven days a week from 11am till late, to eat in or take out. Takeout is pick-up only, so call us on 07 4041 6651.
