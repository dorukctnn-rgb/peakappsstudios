What is Pulcinella?

Originating from commedia dell’arte in the 17th century, Pulcinella is a famous Neapolitan character from classical Italian puppetry, well known for expressing the lively and strong willed nature of the people of Naples.

All our desserts are made in house daily from recipes directly from the motherland, Naples, Italy. Sfogliatella, cannoli, millefoglie, you name it, we have it.
