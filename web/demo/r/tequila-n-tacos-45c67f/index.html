Made daily with love

Tequila n Tacos is a vibrant Mexican restaurant in the heart of Port Noarlunga. Established in 2014, we are proud to be a local favourite, serving fresh, flavour-packed dishes made daily with love and the finest ingredients.

From our famous jalapeño poppers that locals cannot get enough of, to taquitos, potato skins, nachos, enchiladas, sizzling fajitas, pork ribs and steak the Mexican way, there is something for everyone.

Come and support your locally owned, family-run small business.
