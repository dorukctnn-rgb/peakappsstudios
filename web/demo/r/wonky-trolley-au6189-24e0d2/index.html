Michelle and her family

In September 2020, Michelle, the now-managing Director and her family took charge of the existing Wonky Trolley on Errol Street, North Melbourne. When Michelle and her family collected the keys to the location, the first thing they did was keep the doors closed for over a month. This was to ensure that the old burger joint met Michelle’s high standards for cleanliness and healthy, delicious burgers.

At Wonky Trolley, we’re all about community and quality. Each of our beef burgers is named after local streets, celebrating the neighborhood we love. Our most popular creation, the Victoria Burger, is our delicious take on the classic Aussie burger with “the lot”.
