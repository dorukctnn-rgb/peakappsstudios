A family-owned burger shop in Arundel

The original The Burger Shop Co. restaurant is in the heart of the historic town of Arundel, down the hill on the High Street.

We are a family-owned business. We make as much of our menu in house as we can, with fresh, high-quality ingredients, unless a trusted partner can do it better.

Our patties follow our own recipe: 100% British steak with nothing added, dry-aged for at least 28 days by H G Walter, an independent, family-run business established in 1972.
