Baked in house, every day

Located in the heart of Nambour, Downtown Burgers serves burgers made with top-quality ingredients, alongside refreshing drinks and tasty bar bites.

We take pride in preparing our soft pretzel buns in house, along with a whole range of goodies.

Our mission is to become Nambour's burger bar, with a community-first approach and a commitment to celebrating Nambour's history.
