Since 1995, at this same place

From a humble beginning back in 1995 at this same place, Filou’s has been a local suburban patisserie in Carlton North. Over the years it has become a place to indulge in a coffee and croissants, freshly baked breads and pastries, fresh cakes, savoury pies and quiches.

Our food is freshly baked by our experienced and passionate team of chefs daily, and our focus is on producing fresh, tasty food our customers can enjoy. We also plan and cater events, with great care in the preparation and delivery, no matter what the volume.
