How we make it

01 / 04

  1. Simplicity

    Simple Italian influenced toppings, not too much cheese and plenty of flavour. The Napoli way.

  2. Quality ingredients

    Double zero flour, sea salt and tomatoes sourced from Italy. Mozzarella, ricotta and butter made fresh in the UK.

  3. The right oven

    A handmade, traditional Neapolitan wood fired oven, heated by locally sourced sustainable wood.

  4. Passion

    Pizzaioli trained by an Italian World Champion Pizzaiolo.
