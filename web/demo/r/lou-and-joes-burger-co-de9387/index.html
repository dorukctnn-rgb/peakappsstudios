Local, from the first bite

We keep it local: we hand-pick our ingredients from local suppliers and show off the finest produce God's county can create.

All of our beef patties are balled in house by the Lou & Joe's tribe. The beef comes from Irish and Scottish herds and is a mix of sirloin, flank and rump.
