A family bakery since 2014

Halswell Bakery was opened in 2014 by the Buckley family, experienced bakery and business owners. The whole family has been involved in building the business, bringing that family style and feel to the bakery.

Everything is handcrafted with love, right here in the store, and ingredients are sourced locally where possible. In the New World Shopping Centre on Halswell Road there is barista-made coffee, handmade pies, filled rolls, slices and cakes, plus custom cakes and catering for morning teas, lunches, picnics and BBQs.
