A hair transplant takes a year. This is that year.

Tutam is a hair clinic in Antalya. Follow one head from the first consultation to month twelve.

It usually starts at the crown or the temples.

Hair on top thins slowly, over years. The back and sides tend to keep their density, and a transplant works with that difference.

Day 0. Your own hair, moved one follicle at a time.

With FUE, the doctor takes small groups of one to four hairs from the back of the head and places them where the hair has thinned, along a hairline drawn for your face. It is done under local anaesthesia and takes most of a day.

You arrive the day before and stay at the hotel. We drive you to the clinic and back.

Month 1

The new hairs fall out. It looks as if nothing happened, and that is expected: the follicles stay under the skin and rest.

Month 3

Fine new hairs start to come through, thin and uneven at first.

Month 6

Most people see a clear change around here. The hair is thicker, though not yet at full density.

Month 9

The hairline fills in and each hair keeps thickening.

Month 12

This is usually when the result is judged, and some people keep improving until month 18. Results vary from person to person.

It starts with a few photos.

Send photos of your hair. Before you book anything, a doctor tells you whether a transplant suits you and roughly how many grafts it would take.

If you come to Antalya, the package covers

  • Airport and clinic transfers
  • Video check-ins at months 1, 3, 6 and 12
