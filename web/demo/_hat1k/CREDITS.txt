Category drafts (burger, burrito, pizza, bakery, street food): credits and licences

Code
- <category>.css, <category>.js and imlec.js were written for these drafts by Peak Apps Studio. No third-party runtime library is shipped
  (no GSAP, Lenis, three.js or any framework). Techniques only; no code was taken from any other site.
- imlec.js: one 2D canvas over the page. A chain of ingredients follows the pointer and tumbles in 3D as it moves; when the pointer rests
  the chain drops into a stacked product with a springy bounce. Fast movement and clicks throw ingredients that spin, fall with gravity
  and bounce once off the bottom edge. On touch screens a tap bursts ingredients and a fast scroll lets a few fall from the top.
  At most 40 particles from a fixed pool (no allocation while running); the animation loop only runs while something moves.
  Off under prefers-reduced-motion, Save-Data, low device memory and in capture mode.

Art
- Street-food pages (kebab, doner, pide, lahmacun ...): the same as bakery pages, the cursor trail is the business's OWN products cut out from
  its own photos; when the pointer rests they settle on a round serving tray drawn on the canvas. The product slider and the menu photos use
  the same cut-outs. sokak.css and sokak.js only add these sections on top of the unchanged burger.css and burger.js.
- Bakery pages: the cursor trail is the bakery's OWN products. Each page has its own sprite sheet (img/iz-*.webp) cut out from the bakery's
  own product photos (rows = products, columns = 12 steps of a turn, drawn from the cut-out photo). No outside art.
- <category>-3d.webp: ingredient sprite sheet (rows = ingredients, columns = 16 steps of one full turn), modelled procedurally in three.js
  and rendered offline by Peak Apps Studio. No outside models or textures. Released as CC0.

Fonts (see fonts/LICENSES.txt)
- Display and sticker faces are the typefaces of each category's reference page when they are free (SIL Open Font License 1.1),
  self-hosted WOFF2. The business's logo is always its own file, unchanged.

Photos, logos, menu text
- Belong to each business and come from its own public website. Logos are the original files, byte for byte.
- Photos are downsized copies used only inside that business's own unlisted draft (noindex). Nothing is enlarged.
