Dropped kerb in Birmingham: the rules and fees

Birmingham City Council charges £125 for the site inspection and wants a parking space at least 4.75 m deep and 2.75 m wide. The fee is not refunded. If you go ahead, add £375 for the administration fee.

Read from Birmingham City Council’s own pages on 11 October 2026. Birmingham. The city council is both the highway authority and the planning authority.

Check your driveway against Birmingham’s rules

  • Fee: Birmingham City Council charges £125 for the site inspection, not refunded, plus £375 for the administration fee.
  • Space: a parking space at least 4.75 m deep and 2.75 m wide.
  • Junctions: Applications near an existing junction, at or near roundabouts, near traffic signals with regular queues or on fast roads may be refused; no distance is given.
  • Who builds it: Birmingham builds the crossing itself.
  • Decision: The policy promises a response within 25 days for a standard application; the cost pages expect a quote within 28 working days of the inspection, to accept within 2 months. Installation takes at least 16 weeks after payment, over 20 weeks with trees, utilities or street furniture.

Birmingham builds the crossing itself. Only the council can authorise a crossing, and only its authorised contractors build it; you pay in full before work, with no instalments.

Applying, and what happens next

  1. The policy promises a response within 25 days for a standard application; the cost pages expect a quote within 28 working days of the inspection, to accept within 2 months. Installation takes at least 16 weeks after payment, over 20 weeks with trees, utilities or street furniture.
  2. Permission is valid for 12 months from approval; an application stays open 1 year after the inspection.
  3. The policy gives no right of appeal, and the application fee is not refunded.

Planning permission in Birmingham

The highway consent and planning permission are separate. Nationally, an access on to an unclassified road and a porous or drained hard surface are permitted development for a house; Birmingham adds:

  • Birmingham’s policy says planning permission is required for a flat, maisonette or larger shared house, a listed building or a conservation area, a classified road, a verge that is not highway, or over 5 m² of non-permeable driveway. (BCC policy 2026)

Questions

Does Birmingham refund the fee if it refuses?

No. The £125 is not refunded, so check the frontage against the rules first.

Can I use my own contractor in Birmingham?

Only the council can authorise a crossing, and only its authorised contractors build it; you pay in full before work, with no instalments.

How long is a Birmingham approval valid?

Permission is valid for 12 months from approval; an application stays open 1 year after the inspection.
