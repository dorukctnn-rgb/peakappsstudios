Dropped kerb in Surrey: the rules and fees

Surrey County Council charges £383 to apply and wants a parking space at least 4.8 m deep and 2.4 m wide. The fee is not refunded. Re-marking the agreed crossover, if you want it: £116.

Read from Surrey County Council’s own pages on 11 October 2026. Surrey. The 11 district and borough councils decide planning and must confirm it in writing first.

Check your driveway against Surrey’s rules

  • Fee: Surrey County Council charges £383 to apply, not refunded; Re-marking the agreed crossover (optional).
  • Space: a parking space at least 4.8 m deep and 2.4 m wide, 6 m deep in front of a garage.
  • Junctions: Not at busy junctions where it creates conflict between road users.
  • Who builds it: Surrey uses its approved contractors.
  • Decision: An officer visits and marks out the crossover within 20 working days of the application.

Surrey uses its approved contractors. Only the council’s approved contractors may build it. You pay the full contractor quote to the council up front (stage 2); the council inspects the work.

Applying, and what happens next

  1. You must first get written confirmation from your district or borough council of whether planning permission is needed (it may charge, and take up to 6 weeks).
  2. An officer visits and marks out the crossover within 20 working days of the application.
  3. The licence is valid for 6 months.
  4. You can ask for a review by a senior engineer, setting out why the criteria were applied wrongly; the aim is a reply within 10 working days, and that decision is final.

Planning permission in Surrey

The highway consent and planning permission are separate. Nationally, an access on to an unclassified road and a porous or drained hard surface are permitted development for a house; Surrey adds:

  • Surrey says an A, B or C road needs planning permission, and a D road may. (SCC householder guidance)
  • Written evidence from the district or borough council is required either way. (SCC householder guidance)

Also worth knowing in Surrey

  • Owning an electric vehicle does not change the criteria. (SCC crossover page)
  • A ditch or stream beside the road may need a pipe or bridge; the watercourse consent costs £50 and is handled in the process. (SCC householder guidance)

Questions

Does Surrey refund the fee if it refuses?

No. The £383 is not refunded, so check the frontage against the rules first.

Can I use my own contractor in Surrey?

Only the council’s approved contractors may build it. You pay the full contractor quote to the council up front (stage 2); the council inspects the work.

How long is a Surrey approval valid?

The licence is valid for 6 months.
