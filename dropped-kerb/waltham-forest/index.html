Dropped kerb in Waltham Forest: the rules and fees

London Borough of Waltham Forest charges £475 to apply and wants a parking space at least 4.8 m deep and 2.5 m wide. The fee is not refunded. If you go ahead, add £400 for the conditional legal agreement. The crossing must be at least 10 m from a junction.

Read from London Borough of Waltham Forest’s own pages on 11 October 2026. Waltham Forest, except Transport for London’s red routes. The borough is also the planning authority.

Check your driveway against Waltham Forest’s rules

  • Fee: London Borough of Waltham Forest charges £475 to apply, not refunded, plus £400 for the conditional legal agreement.
  • Space: a parking space at least 4.8 m deep and 2.5 m wide.
  • Junctions: at least 10 m from a junction, 8 m at the very least.
  • Who builds it: Waltham Forest builds the crossing itself.
  • Decision: An officer visits and, if suitable, emails an estimate; the frontage must be finished to the policy within 3 months of a provisional approval.

Waltham Forest builds the crossing itself. The council builds every crossover with its term contractor, after full payment and once your front garden works are finished.

Applying, and what happens next

  1. Every applicant signs a conditional legal agreement, registered as a local land charge, that binds later owners.
  2. An officer visits and, if suitable, emails an estimate; the frontage must be finished to the policy within 3 months of a provisional approval.
  3. Estimates are valid for 3 months.
  4. The decision is final; there is no appeal.

Planning permission in Waltham Forest

The highway consent and planning permission are separate. Nationally, an access on to an unclassified road and a porous or drained hard surface are permitted development for a house; Waltham Forest adds:

  • Waltham Forest says planning permission is needed on a classified road, for a listed building, a flat or maisonette, in a conservation area, or for more than 5 m² of non-permeable surface; it can advise when you apply. (LBWF dropped kerbs page)

The national planning rules for dropped kerbs

Also worth knowing in Waltham Forest

  • If any part of a car sits beside the fully flat section of a dropped kerb, a penalty charge can be issued; not beside the sloping part alone. (LBWF dropped kerbs page)
  • Driving over a kerb without a crossover can lead to a formal notice; ignoring it risks a fine of up to £1,000 plus the council’s costs. (LBWF dropped kerbs page)

Questions

Does Waltham Forest refund the fee if it refuses?

No. The £475 is not refunded, so check the frontage against the rules first.

Can I use my own contractor in Waltham Forest?

The council builds every crossover with its term contractor, after full payment and once your front garden works are finished.

How long is a Waltham Forest approval valid?

Estimates are valid for 3 months.
