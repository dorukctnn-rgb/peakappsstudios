Tools that finish the fiddly part of the job

Each one does a single thing that usually takes Photoshop, a paid subscription or an afternoon of spreadsheet work. They run in your browser, so your designs, books, patterns and payroll files never leave your computer.

For makers and Etsy sellers

Templates and files you print or cut at true size, and your year on Etsy in numbers.

  • Tumbler template generator

    Enter the top and bottom diameter and height of any tapered tumbler, cup or mug and get an exact flat template. Then bend your design to fit it, ready to print.

    FreePro $19 once

  • Cone and lampshade template

    The same geometry for paper cones, truncated cones and drum-free lampshades, with an optional glue tab. Free, printed at true size.

    FreePro $19 once

  • KDP coloring book interior maker

    Drop in your coloring pages and get an interior PDF with Amazon KDP's bleed, gutter margins and blank backs worked out for your page count. Free for books up to 24 coloring pages.

    FreePro $19 once

  • Print only your size

    Open a layered PDF sewing pattern, switch off the sizes you don't need and print or save just yours, even on a Mac, iPad or Chromebook.

    FreePro $9 once

  • Quilt label maker

    Design a label with a quilt-block border and print it at true size on an inkjet fabric sheet. Includes a Quilt of Valor checklist.

    FreePro $9 once

  • Etsy tax summary

    Drop your Etsy monthly statement CSVs and get the year's sales, refunds and every fee, with the Schedule C line or Self Assessment box beside each total.

    FreePro $19 once

  • Name cut files

    Paste a list of names or an Etsy orders file and get welded, same-size SVG cut files laid out on a 12x12 or 12x24 mat for Cricut or Silhouette.

    FreePro $15 once

For HR, payroll and employees

Reporting under UK, Irish and EU rules, worked out from your own files, and a letter for employees who need changes at work.

  • Pay gap report

    Drop a payroll export and get the statutory gender pay gap measures for the UK, Ireland or the EU Pay Transparency Directive, with the data checked first and nothing uploaded.

    FreePro $79 once

  • Pay transparency kit

    Workers write the request for their pay information; employers answer it from a payroll export. With the EU directive's status in all 27 countries, checked on official sources.

    FreeFor employers, Pro $99 once

  • Job evaluation

    Score each role on skills, effort, responsibility and working conditions with the factor plan of the EU-wide guidelines, and see which jobs are work of equal value. With bias checks, and the categories ready for the pay gap report.

    Free up to 5 rolesPro $99 once

  • Equality action plan

    Build a gender pay gap and menopause action plan from GOV.UK’s 18 actions, with the minimums and the 100 and 200 word limits checked as you write, and a diagnosis of your published figures.

    FreePro $79 once

  • Menopause adjustments at work

    For employees: the changes Acas and GOV.UK list for employers, chosen by symptom and each with its source, and a polite letter asking your manager to talk about them.

    FreeNo Pro version

For homeowners

A home project checked against the national planning rules for England and Ireland, and a driveway against your council’s dropped kerb rules.

  • Without Planning

    Draw your house, garden and project and check it in 3D against England’s permitted development rules and Ireland’s exemptions of 27 July 2026, condition by condition with the legal reference for each.

    FreePro $29 once

  • Dropped Kerb Check

    Check a driveway in 3D against your council’s dropped kerb rules: the depth and width of the space, the splays, junctions, trees and street lights, with the planning answer and the fees, from each council’s own pages of 11 October 2026.

    FreePro $19 once, per property
