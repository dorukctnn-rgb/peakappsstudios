Privacy and licenses

Last updated 28 October 2026 by Peak Apps Studio. Contact: info@peakappsstudio.com

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on these pages, including the tumbler and cone templates, the KDP coloring book interior maker, the sewing pattern printer, the quilt label maker, the Etsy tax summary, the pay gap report, the pay transparency kit, the job evaluation, the equality action plan, the menopause adjustments tool, Without Planning, Dropped Kerb Check, Catio Designer, Coop Designer and Sim Rig Designer, processes your images, PDFs, CSV and spreadsheet files inside your browser with JavaScript. The files you open are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and closing the tab discards them.

The pay gap report reads payroll exports, which hold personal data. It keeps nothing from them in your browser’s storage: the figures, the PDF report and the CSV files are built on the page and exist only in the tab and in the files you choose to download.

The pay transparency kit works the same way. The request letter is written on the page from what you type, and nothing you type is sent or stored. The employer answer tool reads the payroll export in the tab; its request log is a CSV file you download and can drop back in later, and the tool keeps no copy of it.

The job evaluation keeps the roles, the scores and their reasons in the tab only. Any payroll export you merge with it is read on the page, and the merged file is built there. Saving the evaluation downloads a file to your computer that you can open again later; we keep no copy.

The equality action plan saves the plan you are writing (the organisation name, the actions, their text, the published figures you type in and the measures tracker) in your browser’s local storage, so you can come back to it on the same computer; Start over deletes it. The HR export for the Pro analysis is read on the page and not stored, and the survey answers you type into the consultation kit stay in the tab until you download them.

The menopause adjustments tool stores nothing: what you choose and the letter you write exist only in the tab, because they are about your health.

Without Planning works out the check and draws the 3D model in the tab, from the sizes and answers you type; it asks for no address and stores none of it. Its share link puts the project in the part of the web address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to servers; anyone you give the link to can see the project. The PDF report and the letters are built on the page.

Dropped Kerb Check works the same way. The check and the 3D street are worked out in the tab from the measurements and answers you type; you choose your council from a list, and the tool asks for no address or postcode and stores none of it. Its share link puts the frontage in the part of the web address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to servers. The application pack, the letters and the checklists are built on the page.

Catio Designer works the same way. The design, the checks, the shopping list and the 3D model are worked out in the tab from the sizes and choices you type, and any prices you type stay in the tab. Its share link puts the whole design, prices included, in the part of the web address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to servers. For the shade check it can look up a latitude from a city name: that list (from GeoNames, under CC BY 4.0) is a file on this site, fetched the first time you use the box, and what you type is not sent anywhere. Saved designs (Pro) are kept in your browser’s local storage only. The build plan PDF and the 3D files are made on the page.

Sim Rig Designer works the same way. Your height and inside leg, your gear and the hole patterns you measure, the design, the checks, the shopping list and the 3D model are worked out in the tab from what you type, and any prices you type stay in the tab. Its share link puts the whole design in the part of the web address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to servers. Saved designs (Pro) are kept in your browser’s local storage only. The build plan PDF, the DXF files and the 3D files are made on the page.

Coop Designer works the same way. The flock, the yard, the design, the checks, the shopping list and the 3D model are worked out in the tab from the numbers and choices you type, and any prices you type stay in the tab. Its share link puts the whole design, prices included, in the part of the web address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to servers. It asks for no address or location. Saved designs (Pro) are kept in your browser’s local storage only. The build plan PDF and the 3D files are made on the page.

Lastleaf, the Yahoo Notepad file reader, opens the .json and .zip files Yahoo emails you inside the tab. Your notes, their titles, notebooks and dates are not uploaded, not kept in your browser’s storage and not sent anywhere; closing the tab discards them, and the text file you save is made on the page. It asks for no Yahoo password or sign-in, has no Pro and loads no licence code. When it cannot tell which fields hold the notes, its structure report lists only the names of the fields in your file and how many there are, with no note text, titles, notebook names, dates or file names; it reaches your clipboard only when you press its button, and whether you send it to us is up to you.

Skillcase, the custom GPT and Gemini Gem export, works inside the tab too. What you paste (a GPT's or a Gem's name, description, instructions, conversation starters and action schemas), the knowledge files you add and the ChatGPT data export you open are read on the page and are not uploaded, not kept in your browser's storage and not sent anywhere; closing the tab discards them. From an export it reads only the conversations file and keeps only the chats with GPTs and projects, in memory, until you close the export or the tab. The backup, the skill files and the Modelfile are made on the page, including the text it takes from a Word file for a Gemini skill. It asks for no OpenAI or Google password, has no Pro and loads no licence code.

Stashback, the Edge Collections file reader, works inside the tab too. The collectionsSQLite file you open is read on the page and is not uploaded, not kept in your browser's storage and not sent anywhere; closing the tab discards it. The pages, notes and pictures it shows, the bookmarks file, the spreadsheet and the printout are made on the page. Opening a saved page's address is up to you: it opens that website in a new tab, as any link does. It asks for no Microsoft password or sign-in, has no Pro and loads no licence code.

Rehoused, the Weebly archive tool, works inside the tab too. The archive ZIP (or the unzipped folder) you open is read on the page, in a background worker of the same page, and is not uploaded, not kept in your browser's storage and not sent anywhere; closing the tab discards it. The preview of each page is drawn in a frame on the page with scripts switched off, using pictures taken from your own archive. The clean website, its report and the sample archive's files are made on the page. It asks for no Weebly or Square password or sign-in, has no Pro and loads no licence code. The two outside addresses a guide page links to (Weebly's help pages) open only if you click them.

Unframe, the Publisher .pub file reader, works inside the tab too. The .pub file (or the folder of them) you open is read on the page, in a background worker of the same page, and is not uploaded, not kept in your browser's storage and not sent anywhere; closing the tab discards it. The text and pictures it shows, and the Word file, text, Markdown, web page and ZIPs it saves, are made on the page. Clip art stored as WMF or EMF is drawn on the page by two scripts that belong to this site and is turned into a PNG there. Opening a link stored in a file is up to you: Unframe lists the addresses as plain text and makes none of them clickable. It asks for no Microsoft sign-in, has no Pro and loads no licence code.

What is stored in your browser

We keep a few settings in your browser's local storage so the tools remember them: for example your last measurements, trim size or unit. If you activate Pro, the license key and the date it was last checked are stored there too. Costs you type into the Etsy tax summary are kept in local storage (under etsy-tax:costs) and are never sent anywhere. The equality action plan keeps your draft plan under equality-action-plan:draft. The job evaluation remembers only whether you chose to hide the running total while scoring (under job-evaluation:hide-total). Catio Designer keeps the designs you save with Pro under catio-designer:saved, Coop Designer under coop-designer:saved, and Sim Rig Designer under sim-rig-designer:saved. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

When you open one of these pages, it also notes what kind of site sent you, as one word such as "google", "ai-assistant", "pinterest", "social", "referral", or "direct" when no site did. A tag in the address you arrived at, such as the utm_source=chatgpt.com that ChatGPT adds to its links, counts before the site you came from. The word for this visit is kept in session storage (under peak-source:visit) and is deleted when you close the tab. The first word other than "direct" is also kept in local storage (under peak-source:first) until you clear your browser data. Only the word is kept, never the address of the page you came from, and it goes nowhere unless you click a Pro button, as explained below. The menopause adjustments tool does not note it.

Pro licenses and Gumroad

Pro is sold through Gumroad, which handles payment, receipts, taxes and refunds under its own privacy policy. When you activate Pro, your browser sends the license key to Gumroad's license API to confirm the purchase; we receive no card details. The key is re-checked about once a week, and Pro switches off if a purchase is refunded.

When you click a Pro button, the link to Gumroad carries three labels: that word (the first one this browser kept, or else this visit’s), its group (search, referral, social or none) and the name of the tool. Gumroad stores them with the sale, so we can see which kinds of sites bring buyers: a search engine, an AI assistant, Pinterest, a social network, another website or none. Gumroad learns only the kind of site, never its address. Until you click, the word stays in your browser.

We also keep a small counter of our own, to see where visitors stop before Gumroad. When you click a Pro button, or a tool opens its Pro window by itself, your browser sends one short note to a Cloudflare Worker that we run (peak-funnel.railer-iot.workers.dev). The note holds five things: what happened (a Pro button was clicked, the Pro window opened, or you opened the box where you type a key), the tool, the address of the page without anything after a ? or #, the part of the page the button is in (for example dialog or section#pricing) and the one word for the kind of site that sent you. The Worker adds one to a daily total for that combination and drops the note. It sets no cookie and stores no IP address, no browser details, no time of day and nothing that tells one visitor from another, so a note cannot be traced to you or joined to your other visits. Daily totals are deleted after 400 days. Nothing is sent when you only open or read a page or when your browser is under automation, and nothing you open or type in a tool is ever part of a note. A few pages that pull search traffic are left as they were and run no counter at all.

A Pro license is for one person. You may use it on your own computers and use what you make with the tools in products you sell. A Pay Gap Report Pro license covers one organisation: the people preparing that organisation’s reports may use it. A Pay Transparency Kit Pro license also covers one organisation: the people answering that organisation’s pay information requests may use it. A Job Evaluation Pro license covers one organisation too: the people carrying out and reviewing that organisation’s job evaluation may use it. An Equality Action Plan Pro license covers one organisation and every reporting entity in it: the people preparing, approving and reviewing its action plans may use it. A Dropped Kerb Check Pro license covers one property: the packs and letters for that address. A Catio Designer Pro license is for one person and every catio they design with it; a Coop Designer Pro license, for one person and every coop they design with it; a Sim Rig Designer Pro license, for one person and every rig they design with it.

Visit counts and cookies

We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no personal data, and never your files. Its script is on most pages of these tools. When a page loads, and again when you leave it, the script sends Cloudflare the address of the page without anything after a ? or #, the page you came from (cut the same way), your browser and system type and the page’s loading times. It sets no cookies and keeps nothing in your browser. Nothing you open, type or save in a tool is part of that report, and the web address after a # sign, where the share links keep a design, is never sent. Cloudflare says that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data (Cloudflare Web Analytics, About, checked 21 October 2026). We use the numbers to see which pages people open and where they leave, so we can fix what is confusing. Clicks on Pro buttons are counted separately, by the counter described under Pro licenses and Gumroad.

These pages set no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and the page requested) for security and reliability.
