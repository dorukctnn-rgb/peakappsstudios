Privacy and licenses

Last updated 10 October 2026 by Peak Apps Studio. Contact: peakappsstudios@gmail.com

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on these pages, including the tumbler and cone templates, the KDP coloring book interior maker, the sewing pattern printer, the quilt label maker, the Etsy tax summary, the pay gap report, the pay transparency kit, the job evaluation, the equality action plan and the menopause adjustments tool, processes your images, PDFs, CSV and spreadsheet files inside your browser with JavaScript. The files you open are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and closing the tab discards them.

The pay gap report reads payroll exports, which hold personal data. It keeps nothing from them in your browser’s storage: the figures, the PDF report and the CSV files are built on the page and exist only in the tab and in the files you choose to download.

The pay transparency kit works the same way. The request letter is written on the page from what you type, and nothing you type is sent or stored. The employer answer tool reads the payroll export in the tab; its request log is a CSV file you download and can drop back in later, and the tool keeps no copy of it.

The job evaluation keeps the roles, the scores and their reasons in the tab only. Any payroll export you merge with it is read on the page, and the merged file is built there. Saving the evaluation downloads a file to your computer that you can open again later; we keep no copy.

The equality action plan saves the plan you are writing (the organisation name, the actions, their text, the published figures you type in and the measures tracker) in your browser’s local storage, so you can come back to it on the same computer; Start over deletes it. The HR export for the Pro analysis is read on the page and not stored, and the survey answers you type into the consultation kit stay in the tab until you download them.

The menopause adjustments tool stores nothing: what you choose and the letter you write exist only in the tab, because they are about your health.

What is stored in your browser

We keep a few settings in your browser's local storage so the tools remember them: for example your last measurements, trim size or unit. If you activate Pro, the license key and the date it was last checked are stored there too. Costs you type into the Etsy tax summary are kept in local storage (under etsy-tax:costs) and are never sent anywhere. The equality action plan keeps your draft plan under equality-action-plan:draft. The job evaluation remembers only whether you chose to hide the running total while scoring (under job-evaluation:hide-total). Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Pro licenses and Gumroad

Pro is sold through Gumroad, which handles payment, receipts, taxes and refunds under its own privacy policy. When you activate Pro, your browser sends the license key to Gumroad's license API to confirm the purchase; we receive no card details. The key is re-checked about once a week, and Pro switches off if a purchase is refunded.

A Pro license is for one person. You may use it on your own computers and use what you make with the tools in products you sell. A Pay Gap Report Pro license covers one organisation: the people preparing that organisation’s reports may use it. A Pay Transparency Kit Pro license also covers one organisation: the people answering that organisation’s pay information requests may use it. A Job Evaluation Pro license covers one organisation too: the people carrying out and reviewing that organisation’s job evaluation may use it. An Equality Action Plan Pro license covers one organisation and every reporting entity in it: the people preparing, approving and reviewing its action plans may use it.

No tracking cookies

These pages set no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and the page requested) for security and reliability.

Open-source software

The tools use pdf.js (Apache License 2.0) to read PDFs, pdf-lib (MIT License) to write them and JSZip (MIT License) to package batch downloads. The name cut file tool also uses opentype.js (MIT License) and Clipper (Boost Software License). The lettering in the label and name tools uses fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Their licence texts ship with the tools.
