KDP spine width calculator

Spine width is the page count times the thickness of one page. KDP publishes the factors: 0.002252 in per page for white paper, 0.0025 in for cream, 0.00235 in for groundwood, 0.002347 in for premium color and 0.002252 in for standard color. The full cover is the back, spine and front side by side, plus 0.125 in of bleed all round.

Full cover size

KDP's formulas:

Cover width = 0.125 + back width + spine + front width + 0.125
Cover height = 0.125 + trim height + 0.125

Worked example: an 8.5 × 11 in coloring book with 50 designs on blank backs has 100 pages. On white paper the spine is 100 × 0.002252 = 0.2252 in, so the cover is 0.125 + 8.5 + 0.2252 + 8.5 + 0.125 = 17.475 × 11.25 in, or 5243 × 3375 pixels at 300 dpi.

The cover is always one PDF with back, spine and front together, and every cover has bleed, even when the interior doesn't.

The 79-page rule for spine text

KDP only allows text on the spine from 79 pages. Below that, a cover with spine text is rejected; keep the spine to a color or a pattern. KDP's wording differs slightly between pages: the cover guide says “at least 79 pages”, the submission guidelines say “more than 79”, and its Cover Creator needs 80. A book of 80 pages or more is safe under all three.

When text is allowed, keep 0.0625 in between the text and each spine fold. KDP also allows for 0.0625 in of variance either side of the fold lines, so avoid hard edges or thin borders right on a fold.

With blank backs, a coloring book reaches 80 pages at 40 designs.

Barcode and safe areas

KDP adds a barcode to the back cover if you don't supply one. Leave room for it: KDP's suggested barcode size is 2 × 1.2 in on a white background, at least 0.25 in from the spine and the trim. Keep front and back cover text at least 0.125 in inside the trim lines, and out of the spine area.

Barcode rules: KDP Barcodes, checked 7 October 2026.

Groundwood paper

Groundwood is a lighter (45 lb), lower-cost paper for black-and-white interiors; KDP puts its per-page printing cost about 5% below white and cream. Per page it is slightly thicker than white and thinner than cream: KDP's submission guidelines give 0.00235 in. If you switch an existing book to groundwood, KDP warns that books over 350 pages (from cream) or 525 pages (from white) need a new spine size on the cover.

Source: KDP Groundwood Paper.
