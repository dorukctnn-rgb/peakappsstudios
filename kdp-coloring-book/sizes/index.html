Trim size guide

KDP coloring book sizes

Amazon KDP prints paperbacks in 16 trim sizes for Amazon.com, from 5 × 8 in up to 8.5 × 11 in and A4. For a coloring book the choice comes down to three things: how much room each design gets, how many pages the size allows on your paper, and what it costs to print.

What the size changes

Room for each design

8.5 × 11 in gives the largest drawing area of the US sizes, about 7.9 × 10.5 in inside KDP's margins. 8.5 × 8.5 in keeps the width and makes the page square, which suits mandalas and other round designs. At 6 × 9 in it drops to 5.4 × 8.5 in, a little over half the area of letter size.

Pixels you need

KDP asks for 300 DPI at the printed size. A design filling the 8.5 × 11 in drawing area needs about 2363 × 3150 pixels; at 6 × 9 in, about 1613 × 2550. Pages drawn for printable letter-size sheets fit 8.5 × 11 in without resizing.

Page limits

The square and letter sizes (8.5 × 8.5 and 8.5 × 11 in) top out at 590 pages on white paper and 550 on cream, where most trims allow 828 and 776. Coloring books rarely get close, even with blank backs. Standard color starts at 72 pages and isn't offered for A4.

Print cost on Amazon.com

KDP charges a fixed cost per book plus a cost per page, and large trims cost more. A trim counts as large when it is wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in, so 8.5 × 11 in is large and 6 × 9 in is regular.

Matching the size to the book

  • Big, simple shapes for young children: 8.5 × 11 in or 8.5 × 8.5 in, so thick outlines and large areas stay large.
  • Detailed adult designs: 8.5 × 11 in gives fine lines the most room; check that the thinnest lines are at least 0.75 pt, KDP's minimum line weight.
  • Round or symmetrical designs: 8.5 × 8.5 in or 8.25 × 8.25 in wastes less paper above and below the art.
  • Wide scenes: 8.25 × 6 in is KDP's only landscape trim; it binds on the short side.
  • Travel or pocket books: 6 × 9 in or 5.5 × 8.5 in cost less per copy but give a much smaller page.

Sizes in the interior maker

The free version of the KDP coloring book interior maker lays out 8.5 × 11 in books. Pro adds the other 15 sizes; you can preview any of them for free with the “Preview” links in the table. Each layout uses that size's page with bleed, gutter band and page limits.
