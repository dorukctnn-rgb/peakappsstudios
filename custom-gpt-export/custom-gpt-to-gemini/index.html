Move a custom GPT to Gemini: make a skill, not a Gem

The obvious home for a custom GPT in Gemini used to be a Gem. Google is retiring Gems on personal accounts too, and turning them into skills. So the place to move a GPT now is a Gemini skill, and Gemini takes the same SKILL.md format as Claude and ChatGPT.

Last checked: October 27, 2026, against the companies' own help pages. Not affiliated with OpenAI.

Gems are going away as well

Google's dates for the move from Gems to skills
AccountsWhat happens
Personal, 18 or overNovember 17, 2026: Gems turn into skills and become read-only. December 17, 2026: Gems are removed from Gemini Apps.
Personal, under 18A later date, not yet given
Workspace business, enterprise and non-profitMarch 2027
Workspace educationJune 2027

Google says it converts Gems to skills automatically, but that some files do not carry over: audio and video, legacy Office files (.doc, .xls, .ppt) and archives (About the transition from Gems to skills). A GPT turned into a Gem today would have to move again within weeks. If you have Gems of your own, see what Google's move leaves behind.

Upload a GPT as a Gemini skill

  1. Pack your GPT with Skillcase and download "Skill for Gemini". Its SKILL.md sits at the top of the ZIP, with the knowledge files Gemini takes in references/ and Word files turned into text.
  2. In the Gemini app, create a skill by uploading. Google says you can pick a SKILL.md file, or a folder or .zip with a SKILL.md file in its main folder.
  3. Review the skill and click Create. If the ZIP is refused, unzip it and upload the folder, or the SKILL.md file alone.

Gemini's rules for skill files

  • You must be 18 or over, signed in, with Keep Activity turned on.
  • Plain-text files, PDFs and images are supported; binary formats such as .docx, .doc and .xlsx are not. Skillcase turns Word (.docx) files into text for the Gemini skill and lists any other file it leaves out.
  • Uploaded files must total no more than 100 MB.
  • The skill name must be lowercase, with words separated by hyphens.
  • Scripts that need internet access are not supported. A GPT action calls an outside service, so it will not run from a skill; its schema is kept in the backup.
  • To change a skill's files later, you upload the whole skill again.

All of these are from Google's page, Create and manage skills for Gemini Apps.

What about the GPT's chats?

Gemini skills hold instructions and reference files, not chat history. Keep your chats in the backup's chats folder; see how to save your custom GPT chats.

Questions

Can I turn a custom GPT into a Gemini Gem?

You can still make a Gem on some accounts, but Google is retiring Gems on personal accounts: they become read-only on November 17, 2026 and are removed on December 17, 2026, as they turn into skills. Make a Gemini skill instead.

Do Gemini skills need a paid plan?

Google says skills are available without a Google AI subscription; tasks and schedules need a subscription that supports them. You must be 18 or over with Keep Activity on.

Why is my Word file a .md file in the Gemini skill?

Google says skills take plain-text files, PDFs and images, but not .docx files. Skillcase turns each Word file into Markdown (its text, headings, lists and tables) for the Gemini skill, and keeps the original in the backup's knowledge folder and in the skill for Claude and ChatGPT.
