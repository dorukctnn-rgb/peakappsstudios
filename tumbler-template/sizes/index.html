How this chart was made

Every size comes from a page published by the company that makes or sells the blank: product pages, spec tables or the supplier’s own template chart. Numbers are copied as published, in inches. We left out sizes from template sellers and social posts, and brand pages whose dimensions include the handle and lid, because they don’t tell you the wrap size. Pages change, so each row links to its source. Last checked 7 October 2026.

Brand and product names identify the published source only. Peak Apps is not affiliated with any of these companies.

Questions

What size is a 20 oz skinny tumbler template?

MakerFlo publishes 9.325 × 8.125 in for its straight 20 oz skinny tumbler, printed on 8.5 × 11 in paper. Other brands differ: JPPlus lists its straight 20 oz skinny as 3 in across and 8 1/2 in tall, which is about 9.42 in around. More on the 20 oz skinny page.

What size is a 40 oz tumbler template?

Handled 40 oz tumblers usually take two pieces. MakerFlo publishes Top: 12.9 × 5.75 and Bottom: 10.3 × 3.6 (tapered) for its 40 oz tumbler, on 8.5 × 14 in paper. See the 40 oz guide for making both pieces for your own cup.

What size is an 11 oz mug sublimation template?

Condé lists the imprintable area of its 11 oz DyeTrans mug as 8 × 3.75 in, and Coastal Business Supplies lists 8.3 × 3.9 in for its 11 oz mug with a colored rim and handle. Both leave room for the handle.

Why doesn’t a published size fit my tumbler?

Each size is for one supplier’s blank. Cups sold as the same capacity differ in diameter, height and taper between brands and sometimes between batches, so measure your own blank and make a template from those numbers.
