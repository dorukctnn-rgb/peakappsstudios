What a conical warp does

A tapered cup is a slice of a cone, and unrolled flat its surface is a curved band. To look straight on the cup, a design has to be bent into that band: horizontal lines become arcs around the cone’s tip, vertical lines point at the tip, and the design narrows toward the bottom in step with the cup.

Skip the warp and a rectangle wrapped on a tapered cup tilts: text climbs toward the seam and the ends don’t meet. This tool does the bend from your measurements, so the top of the design follows the top edge exactly and the bottom follows the bottom edge.

How to warp a design here

  1. Enter the cup’s measurements: top diameter, bottom diameter and the height of the area you print. Or open a size from the size chart.
  2. Add the flat design. The tool shows the best size for it: the length of the top edge by the length of the side, at 300 dpi. A design made at that size bends with no stretching along the rim.
  3. Choose how it fits if your design has a different shape: Stretch fills the template, Fit keeps all of it, Fill trims the edges.
  4. Tick Mirror for sublimation, and add a bleed if you want the print to run past the cut line.
  5. Download the 300 dpi PNG (clear outside the shape) or the PDF, and print at 100% (“Actual size”).

Questions

Can Cricut Design Space warp an image for a tapered tumbler?

Cricut’s help center says Warp is an exclusive feature for Cricut Access subscribers, and that the only thing you cannot warp is a printable image for Print Then Cut. A sublimation or print-then-cut design is a printable image, so it can’t be warped there.

Is this conical warp tool free?

Yes. Warping one design at a time and downloading it as a 300 dpi PNG or PDF is free, with no watermark and no sign-up. Pro ($19, one-time) adds batch warping to a ZIP.

Why does my design get narrower at the bottom?

Because the bottom of a tapered cup is smaller around than the top. A design that wraps all the way round has to shrink sideways in the same proportion toward the bottom. On a cup 3.5 in across the top and 2.75 in across the bottom, the bottom row is 21% narrower.
