How to use it

  1. Measure where the print goes. Top and bottom outside diameter (or circumference) and the height between them. Skip the lid, the lip and any base bevel the wrap won’t cover. Measuring guide.
  2. Check the shape. The preview on the mat is drawn to scale. A straight cup gives a rectangle; a tapered one gives a curved band, and the readout shows every length you might want to check with a ruler.
  3. Download the template as SVG for a cutting machine or laser, PDF to print, or PNG at 300 dpi. Print PDFs at 100%, then check the scale square.
  4. Bend your design. Add a flat PNG or JPG and it is warped into the same curve. Tick Mirror for sublimation, then download the 300 dpi PNG or PDF.

How the template is worked out

A tapered cup is a slice of a cone. Unrolled, the slice lies flat as part of a ring, centred on the cone’s tip. With top diameter D1, bottom diameter D2 and straight-up height h:

s = √(h² + ((D1 − D2) ÷ 2)²) side (slant) length R = s × D1 ÷ (D1 − D2) outer radius, tip to top edge r = R − s inner radius, tip to bottom edge θ = 180° × (D1 − D2) ÷ s angle of the ring slice

Worked example: a cup 3.5 in across the top, 2.75 in across the bottom and 4 in tall. The side is 4.018 in, the outer radius 18.749 in, the inner radius 14.731 in and the angle 33.60°. The top edge then measures 10.996 in, which is π × 3.5, and the bottom edge 8.639 in, which is π × 2.75, so the paper wraps exactly once at both ends. The whole template needs a 10.84 × 4.65 in piece of paper. Open this example.

When the top and bottom are the same, the template is a plain rectangle π × D wide. A handle gap shortens the top edge by the gap and the bottom edge in proportion, so the ends stay straight seams. The bent design uses the same geometry in reverse: every pixel of the template is traced back to the point of your flat design it came from, so the top of the design follows the top edge and the bottom follows the bottom edge.

Questions

What measurements do I need for a tapered tumbler template?

Three: the outside diameter where your design should start at the top, the outside diameter where it should end at the bottom, and the height between those two lines. You can enter circumferences instead of diameters, and the length along the side instead of the straight-up height. The measuring guide shows where to put the tape.

Why is a tapered tumbler template curved?

A tapered cup is a slice of a cone. When you unroll a cone it lies flat as part of a ring, so the top and bottom edges become arcs around the cone’s tip. The bigger the difference between the top and bottom diameter, the stronger the curve.

How do I make sure the PDF prints at the right size?

Print at 100% or “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”. The template PDF has a 1 in (or 2 cm) square on it; measure it before you print a batch.

Should I mirror my design for sublimation?

Usually yes: the transfer paper goes face down on the cup, so text and one-sided designs need to be printed mirrored. Some printer software mirrors for you, so check that you don’t mirror twice. Tick Mirror before you download the bent design.

Yes. Templates in SVG, PDF and 300 dpi PNG and bending a design are free with no watermark and no sign-up. Pro is a one-time $19 purchase that adds printing across several sheets, batch bending to a ZIP and a library of published blank sizes.
