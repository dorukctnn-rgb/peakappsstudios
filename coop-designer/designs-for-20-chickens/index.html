The design: a walk-in coop 10 ft by 8 ft

Twenty hens is a small flock by farm standards and a big one for a backyard. The coop is a 10 ft by 8 ft shed with 80 sq ft of floor, 4 sq ft a hen, and the run is the largest part of the build: 25 ft by 8 ft, 200 sq ft at 10 sq ft a hen.

What changes at twenty

  • The yard. The whole build is 35 ft by 8 ft, before the room you need to walk round it and open the doors. Type your yard into the tool first: if the space is shorter, it shortens the run and the space check shows what that costs each hen.
  • Three roost bars. Twenty hens at 8 to 10 in each need 160 to 200 in of bar (Virginia Tech). Three bars of 95 in give 285 in, 14 in a hen, 12 in apart.
  • Five nest boxes. One per four hens (Oregon State); four would do at one per five (Maine), and Penn State's rule asks for 6.
  • Plywood becomes the big order. 18 sheets cover the floor, the walls and the boxes of a house this size, and 8 corrugated panels the roof.

Hot summers. The same walk-in with the climate set to hot adds a hardware-cloth window for summer air. Chickens cope worse with heat than with cold, and can die at 104 F (Colorado State); give them shade in the run and plenty of water.

A hen house on legs stretched to twenty is 13 ft 6 in long and 6 ft deep: the same 81 sq ft from 98 boards, but you clean a house that long from outside through one clean-out door 48 in wide, and its run is 34 ft long.

A tractor fails. A tractor small enough to roll gives twenty hens 3.9 sq ft of run each, 78.7 sq ft in all, under the 4 sq ft a bird that is the smallest figure any guide gives. Past about 7 hens, use two tractors or a fixed run.

Questions

How big should a coop be for 20 chickens?

60 to 80 sq ft inside and 200 sq ft of run at Virginia Tech's 3 to 4 and 10 sq ft a hen; this design has 80 sq ft and 200 sq ft.

How many nesting boxes for 20 chickens?

Four at one per five (Maine), five at one per four (Oregon State), and 6 by Penn State's rule.

Can a chicken tractor hold 20 hens?

Not one that rolls: the tool's tractor for twenty gives 3.9 sq ft of run a hen, under every figure we read. Use two smaller tractors or a fixed run.
