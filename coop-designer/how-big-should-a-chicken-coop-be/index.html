How big should a chicken coop be?

Give each standard hen 3 to 4 sq ft of floor inside the coop and 10 sq ft in the run; give each bantam 2 to 3 sq ft inside and 5 to 8 sq ft outside. Those are the figures in Virginia Tech's 2023 extension table, so six hens need 18 to 24 sq ft of coop and 60 sq ft of run.

Size a coop for your flock

What the extension guides say

No two guides give quite the same number, because some publish minimums and others recommendations. Penn State's table is headed minimum space requirements; Virginia Tech's 2023 table is headed recommended space, and adds that you can never provide too much. Here is what each guide we read says, in its own terms.

Read together, 3 sq ft a hen inside is where most of them meet, and 4 sq ft is the generous end. The 1.5 sq ft minimums come paired with a run of 8 sq ft or more, and Oregon State's 8 to 10 sq ft for hens with no run at all shows how much of the load the run carries. In the run, 10 sq ft a hen is the common recommendation and 4 sq ft the smallest figure anyone gives.

What counts as coop floor

The tool counts the floor inside the hen house walls, where the hens sleep, lay and wait out bad weather. Nest boxes hung on the outside of a wall take none of it, and roost bars above the floor leave it whole. Under a hen house raised on legs, the ground stays part of the run, shaded and dry, so the tool counts it toward the run and tells you how much of the run it is.

Bantams need less, but not half

Bantams are a half to a quarter of the size of a standard chicken (Florida), yet the 2023 table still gives them 2 to 3 sq ft inside, against 3 to 4 for laying hens. Twelve bantams fit a 6 ft x 6 ft hen house on legs with 36 sq ft of floor; twelve standard hens need the 8 ft x 6 ft walk-in with 48 sq ft. Their roosts are shorter and lower too: 4 in of bar a bird, 12 to 18 in above the floor, against 6 in and 18 to 24 in for standard hens (Penn State).

Height

None of the guides sets a height for the house, but they set one for what goes in it. Roosts go 18 to 24 in above the floor and never higher than 4 ft, so heavy hens do not hurt their legs jumping down (Penn State); nest boxes go at least 2 ft off the ground (Penn State). A walk-in coop needs headroom for you instead: the tool makes its run 6 ft tall, with a full-height door.

Your yard decides the rest. Type its size into the tool and it keeps the coop and the run inside it, and says when the flock is too big for the space. Open the design for six hens.

Questions

How big should a chicken coop be for 6 chickens?

18 to 24 sq ft of floor inside and 60 sq ft of run, at Virginia Tech's 3 to 4 and 10 sq ft a hen. The tool builds a 5 ft x 5 ft hen house on legs over a run 12 ft long: 25 sq ft inside and 60 sq ft of run.

How much space do bantams need in a coop?

2 to 3 sq ft each inside and 5 to 8 sq ft in the run (Virginia Tech, 2023). The older minimums are 1 sq ft inside and 4 in the run (Penn State, Maryland).

Does the run count toward coop space?

No. The guides give a figure for inside the house and a separate one for the run. The one exception they make is a coop with no run at all, which needs more room inside: Oregon State gives 8 to 10 sq ft a bird.
