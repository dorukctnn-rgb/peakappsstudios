    Cat enclosure plans in metric

    In Australia and much of the UK a catio is a cat enclosure or a cat run. Switch the tool to metric and it plans the enclosure in millimetres: the frame in 45 x 45 mm, 38 x 63 mm CLS, 38 x 89 mm CLS or 45 x 90 mm timber, cut from lengths between 2.4 m and 4.8 m, and the walls in 13 mm welded mesh from 900 mm rolls.

    • The shade check uses 21 December south of the equator, so a summer cat enclosure in Perth or Sydney gets its shade tested on the longest day there.
    • Australian 90 x 45 mm framing is sold both untreated and treated. Keep treated timber where the cat cannot rub or chew it.
    • Check your council, strata, HOA and lease rules before you build. The tool gives no permit advice.

    Questions

    How big should a catio be?

    No official standard exists. Catios range from cat-sized window boxes to whole rooms. The one published per-cat figure we found is from Catnets, an enclosure maker: at least 2 square metres (about 21.5 sq ft) for each cat when cats share an enclosure. Every cat also needs climbing space and a place to hide. The figures and their sources

    What mesh should I use for a catio?

    Galvanized or stainless welded wire or hardware cloth. Catio Spaces recommends 16 gauge fencing wire with openings no larger than 2 by 3 inches; 1/2 inch hardware cloth in 19 gauge is common and sold for pet runs, but it is lighter. Chicken wire and window screen are not strong enough. Mesh compared

    Can I build a catio with pressure-treated lumber?

    Keep it away from anything the cat can rub or chew. Treated lumber is wood impregnated with a pesticide (US EPA), and old arsenic-treated boards still turn up second hand. Untreated cedar, redwood or pine, with a pet-safe water-based finish if you want one, is the usual choice.

    Is anything I enter sent to a server?

    No. The design, the checks and the 3D model are worked out in your browser. The share link keeps the design after the # sign, which browsers do not send to servers. The city list for the shade check is a file on this site. If you buy Pro, only the licence key is sent to Gumroad to check it. We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no personal data, and never your files.

    Does it work in metric, for a cat enclosure in Australia or the UK?

    Yes. Switch to metric and the frame uses 45 x 45 mm, 38 x 63 mm CLS, 38 x 89 mm CLS or 45 x 90 mm timber in lengths from 2.4 m to 4.8 m, with 13 mm welded mesh in 900 mm rolls.

    Will the catio hold up to snow and wind?

    The tool does not size the frame for snow or wind. Make sure the roof is strong enough for the snowfall you get, and check locally with a builder or your building department if you get heavy snow.
