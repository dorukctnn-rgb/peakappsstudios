How much does it cost to build a catio?

A DIY catio costs what its boards, mesh, panels and screws cost where you live: published figures run from under $100 for a simple catio to thousands of dollars, and a builder quotes about $4,000 for a 6 by 8 ft catio. The lists below give the quantities for three sizes, and you type the prices.

The spread is wide because the materials list changes with every catio. The useful number is your own: the boards, rolls and screws for your size, at your store's prices. What moves the total

  • The lumber. Cedar costs more than pine but resists rot; whichever you choose, keep pressure-treated wood away from anything the cat can rub or chew (US EPA, wood preservatives). A heavier section (2x4 against 2x3) adds wood without adding much strength a catio needs.
  • The mesh. Heavier wire costs more per roll. The roll width decides how many strips you cut: the tool picks the cheaper direction for each wall and counts the rolls.
  • The roof. Mesh is the cheapest top; corrugated panels add the panels, the roofing screws with sealing washers and a slope of at least 5 degrees (Palram, corrugated sheet guide).
  • The door. A walk-in door adds the leaf, a brace, three hinges and a latch.
  • Waste. The sample window box uses 9 boards with 12% of the wood left over. Pro packs every cut onto the boards to waste least and draws each board's cutting diagram.
