MENOPAUSE ACTION PLAN TEMPLATE
The six GOV.UK actions that support employees experiencing menopause. Rules as of 10 October 2026.
Prepared by Peak Apps (peakappsstudio.com/equality-action-plan/menopause-action-plan-template/). Not legal advice.

THE RULES (GOV.UK, Creating an action plan: guidance for employers, Steps 2 and 3)
- Choose at least 1 menopause action and at least 1 gender pay gap action; at least 2 actions must be new or in progress.
- New or in progress actions need up to 100 words: why you chose the action, and how you will track whether it is improving gender equality.
- Embedded actions can have up to 100 words: how you made it part of your working practice, and the results.
- No links and no files in the text. An overall narrative of up to 200 words is optional.
- Voluntary now. GOV.UK says plans will become mandatory “from spring 2027, subject to secondary legislation”.
