1. Pick up to 3 apps

The test links come here. Any address works; what joins the test is the Google account on your phone.

What testing means

  1. An Android phone and the Google account on it.
  2. Join our Google Group once. It is on the list of every test, so it opens all of them.
  3. On each app's test page tap "Become a tester", then install the app from Google Play.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days and open it for a minute on most days.
  5. Reply to any of our mails when something breaks. One line with your phone model helps.

We never ask for reviews, ratings or money. Please do not buy anything inside the apps during the test.

What you get

For apps marked "Lifetime Pro code": after the 14 days, email info@peakappsstudio.com from the Google account you tested with and we send you a lifetime Pro code for that app. There is no other reward and no payment.

How the mails work

Seven mails over the 14 days at most: one to confirm, one with the links, then days 1, 3, 7, 10 and 13. Each brings one small task, what changed in the app when something did, and a "still installed" link that tells us the test is alive. If you tick the box, you also get one mail when a new app opens for testing.

Your data

We keep your e-mail address, your language and time zone, the apps you picked, when you signed up and confirmed, and which "still installed" links you pressed.

It is stored on Cloudflare, and the mails go out through ForwardEmail, our mail provider. We do not share it, sell it or add it to any other list.

Sign-ups that are not confirmed are deleted after 7 days. Unsubscribing deletes your address from the list; we keep it only on a do-not-mail list so it is never mailed again.

Questions, a copy of what we hold or a deletion request: info@peakappsstudio.com. This section is the privacy notice for the tester list.

Questions

Why a Google Group?
Google lets only listed accounts into a closed test. Our group is on the list of every one of our tests, so joining it once opens all of them. In the group settings you can set group mail to "No email".
Google says the app is not available.
Chrome or Play is signed in with a different Google account than the one that joined the group. Switch the account and open the test link again.
Can I test on an iPhone?
No. These are Google Play tests, so they need an Android phone.
Can I stop?
Yes, at any time. Every mail has a one-click unsubscribe, and each test has a leave button on its Google Play test page.
I unsubscribed and want back in.
Write to info@peakappsstudio.com from that address and we put you back.
