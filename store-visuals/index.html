Questions developers ask us.

What do you need from me?

Your App Store or Google Play link, five screenshots of the screens you want shown, and a short line of text for each. Screenshots from your phone or the simulator are fine. If you send no text, we write it from your store listing and you approve it.

Which sizes do I get?

Screenshots at 1290 × 2796 pixels, the iPhone 6.9-inch size. The product page header at 3840 × 1646 and the search result image at 3840 × 2560. The same art also comes as one 5244 × 2950 file.

Where do the header and search images appear?

On the App Store product page and in search results, for apps that are already live. Apple added them on 5 October 2026 and shows them to people on iOS 27 or later. Your screenshots show for everyone.

Do you change my app or my store listing?

No. We make the images. You upload them yourself, and we send a short note that says which file goes where.

Can I have more than one language?

A pack covers one language. If you need more, write to us before you order and we quote it.

Will this bring me more downloads?

Nobody can promise that. Clear screens help a visitor see what the app does within a few seconds, and that is all we claim.

Who are you?

Peak Apps Studio. We build our own apps and games, which is where the samples come from. You can see them on our home page. We work by e-mail, in English.
