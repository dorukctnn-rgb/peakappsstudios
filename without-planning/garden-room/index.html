Garden room without planning permission: the 2.5 m rule and the rest of Class E

In England a garden room needs no planning permission if it is no higher than 2.5 m when any part is within 2 m of a boundary (3 m elsewhere, or 4 m with a dual-pitched roof), its eaves are no higher than 2.5 m, it has one storey, it sits behind the front of the house, and with other buildings it covers no more than half the garden.

Rules as of 10 October 2026. GPDO 2015, Schedule 2, Part 1, Class E.

Check your garden room in 3D

  • Within 2 m of a boundary: no more than 2.5 m high (E.1(e)(ii)).
  • Elsewhere: 4 m with a dual-pitched roof (hipped counts, per the technical guidance), 3 m for any other roof (E.1(e)(i), (iii)).
  • Eaves no higher than 2.5 m, whatever the total height (E.1(f)).
  • Single storey, behind the principal elevation, and with all other buildings no more than half the curtilage (E.1(b) to (d)).
  • For a purpose incidental to the house: not a bedroom, kitchen or separate accommodation.

Home office, gym or annex?

Class E covers a building for a purpose incidental to the enjoyment of the house. The technical guidance gives garden sheds, storage, garages and decking as examples, and says a purpose incidental to the house would not cover separate self-contained accommodation, or primary living accommodation such as a bedroom, bathroom or kitchen. A home office, gym or studio used by the household fits; a bedroom or an annex does not.

Heights are measured from the highest ground next to the building. Decking counts as a raised platform if it is more than 0.3 m high, which Class E does not allow.

Building regulations

  • A detached single-storey building of up to 15 m² with no sleeping accommodation is exempt from the building regulations (Class 6, paragraph 3).
  • Up to 30 m² it is also exempt if it has no sleeping accommodation and is at least 1 m from every boundary or built substantially of non-combustible material (Class 6, paragraph 1).
  • A garden room with sleeping accommodation is not exempt, whatever its size.

Questions

How close to the boundary can a garden room be without planning permission?

Any distance, but if any part is within 2 m of a boundary of the curtilage, the whole building can be no higher than 2.5 m (paragraph E.1(e)(ii)).

What is the biggest garden room without planning permission?

There is no fixed floor area: buildings other than the original house must not cover more than half of the curtilage, excluding the house’s footprint. Height is 2.5 m within 2 m of a boundary, otherwise 3 m, or 4 m with a dual-pitched roof, with eaves no higher than 2.5 m.

Can I sleep in a garden room?

Not under Class E: a bedroom is primary living accommodation, not a purpose incidental to the house. A garden room with sleeping accommodation also needs building regulations approval, whatever its size.
