How to download your end-to-end encrypted Messenger chats

Messenger turned on end-to-end encryption for personal chats, and the regular Download your information export stops where a chat moved. Everything after the move has its own download in Message storage.

Open your download in Readback

Why your newest chats are not in the regular download

Meta cannot read end-to-end encrypted messages on its servers, so the regular export cannot include them. In a Facebook download, chats that moved appear under messages/e2ee_cutover with their history up to the move. The messages after it are kept in your secure storage, encrypted with your PIN or another method you set up, and Messenger gives you a separate download for them.

The steps

  1. Use a computer. Meta's help page describes this download on the web, not in the phone app.
  2. Open your chats on the web. messenger.com stopped working for messages in April 2026 and sends you to facebook.com/messages.
  3. Open the Messenger settings from your profile picture, then Privacy and safety, then End-to-end encrypted chats.
  4. Choose Message storage, then the option to download your secure storage data. Enter your password or PIN if asked.
  5. Keep the page open while the file is prepared; it can take several minutes. The result is a ZIP with one Name_N.json file per chat and a media folder.

Menu names move around between versions, so look for "Message storage" if the labels differ on your screen.

When the download does not work

Common problems and what to try
What happensWhat to try
There is no download optionSecure storage may be off for your account, and then nothing is stored to download. Meta warns that without secure storage your chats live only on your devices. Check Message storage on a computer.
The button does nothing, or the file never arrivesSome people report it only starts in another browser. Try Chrome or Edge, allow downloads for the site, and keep the tab in front until the file is saved.
It asks for a PIN you forgotMeta's help says it cannot reset your PIN for you; you reset it on a device where you created or last entered it.
The ZIP opens but a chat looks shortThat chat's older part is in the regular download, under e2ee_cutover. Choose both downloads together in Readback and they become one timeline.

The December 2026 change for Android backups

Messenger's help page on restoring chats says that after December 2026 you will no longer be able to reach your chat history on a new device with the key saved in your Google Drive; Messenger will ask you to create a PIN instead. If you rely on that key and plan to change phones, set up a PIN first, and keep a downloaded copy of your encrypted chats as well.

Questions

Can I download encrypted Messenger chats on my phone?

Meta's help page gives the steps on a computer. If the option is missing in the app, use a computer.

Are my encrypted chats deleted if I do not download them?

No. They stay in your secure storage and on your devices. The download is a copy you keep yourself, which matters if you lose your PIN or every device you use Messenger on.

Does Readback decrypt anything?

No. Meta decrypts the chats for you when you download them. Readback only reads the file you already have, on your own computer.
