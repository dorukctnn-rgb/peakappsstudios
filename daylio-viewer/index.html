    Get the backup from your phone to the computer

    Daylio makes the backup file by hand, under Advanced options. Its help pages give these steps:

    Android

    1. Open Daylio and tap More, then Backup & Restore. If it asks you to pick a Google account, press back: that is for Google Drive backups.
    2. Scroll to the bottom, tap Advanced options, then Export.
    3. Choose Gmail or another email app and send the backup to an address you can open on the computer.

    iPhone

    1. Open Daylio and go to More, then Backup & Restore.
    2. Tap Advanced options, then Export.
    3. Choose Mail or another email app and send the backup to yourself.

    On the computer

    Open the email, save the attachment and choose it at the top of this page. If the file lost its .daylio ending on the way, Dayfold reads it anyway.

    Questions

    Is there a Daylio app for PC or Mac?

    No. Daylio’s help page says it is not being developed for computers or the web, and suggests an Android emulator such as BlueStacks if you want it on a computer, with a warning that using two devices means a backup and restore every time you switch. Dayfold is not a Daylio app: it reads a backup, so you keep writing on your phone and read on the big screen.

    Is my journal uploaded anywhere?

    No. The backup is opened by JavaScript in your browser tab and is never sent to a server. Photos are read from the file only when they are shown. The only request Pro makes is the license key check with Gumroad. Closing the tab discards everything.

    Can I write or edit entries here?

    No. Dayfold only reads. It never changes your backup and never makes a new one, so nothing you do here reaches the app. To see new entries, export a fresh backup and open it.

    Does it work with backups from an iPhone?

    Yes. Backups from Android and iPhone use the same .daylio format, and Dayfold reads both, with their photos.

    How do I get my Daylio entries as a PDF or CSV?

    Open your backup here. With Pro you can save a PDF journal book with your photos, a CSV with one row per entry and Markdown files for Obsidian, all from the same backup file.

    How are the Year in Pixels colours worked out?

    Each square is a day. With one entry it takes that entry’s mood colour; with several, their average, rounded to the nearest of the five levels. A day without entries stays empty. Custom moods take the colour of the level they belong to.

    What does the free version show?

    Everything for the latest year in your backup: the calendar with every entry and photo, search, mood charts, the Year in Pixels and how often you did each activity. In January and February the year before is free too. Pro opens every other year and the files to save.
