      How to get your Character.AI export

      Character.AI makes the export when you ask for it in your settings, then emails you a link. Its help centre says the export can take 3 to 4 weeks and that the download link works for 7 days after the email is sent.

      In the mobile app

      1. Open your profile.
      2. Tap the Settings icon.
      3. Tap Legal & Privacy.
      4. Tap Export Data. A “Success” banner confirms the request.

      On the website

      1. Go to your profile and click Profile Settings.
      2. Click Account.
      3. Click Manage Account & Data.
      4. Click Export Data. A pop-up confirms the email will follow.

      When to expect it

      Pick the date you tapped Export Data.

      The email and the password

      A hands-on account of a real export (AIInsightsNews, May 2026) shows an email from accounts@character.ai with the subject “Character AI User Data Export”. It links to a ZIP and says: “The password is your lowercase email address.” Download the ZIP while the link works, then open it here and type that address when asked. More on the ZIP password, and what to do if the export isn’t arriving or won’t open.

      Sources: Character.AI Help Center, “How do I download my data?” (checked 21 October 2026); AIInsightsNews, “How to Export Character AI Chat in 2026” (25 May 2026).

      What’s inside the file

      The ZIP holds a folder named after your username, a data folder inside it, and three JSON files.

      FileWhat it holdsHow Chatbound shows it
      message.jsonEvery message, with chat_id, raw_content (the text) and create_timeGrouped into chats by chat_id, in time order
      character.jsonThe characters your account created: name, tagline, description, greeting, definitionThe Characters tab, with each definition ready to copy
      user.jsonYour account: first name, email, sign-up dateThe About this file tab

      The messages have no field that says who sent them or which character a chat was with, so Chatbound shows each chat in time order without guessing names. When a chat starts with the exact greeting of a character you created, the chat says so. The full breakdown is in what’s in the Character.AI export.

      Move your characters and chats to another app

      Open your export, then the Move to another app tab. For each character, Chatbound makes a character card in the Character Card V2 format, the one SillyTavern, Janitor AI, Perchance and Sakura.fm import, as a PNG picture with the card inside it or as a JSON file. Each chat becomes a SillyTavern chat file (JSONL). It all happens on your device, from the export file, so it works without the Character.AI website.

      What doesn’t, because the export doesn’t have it

      • Characters made by other people. The export only defines the characters your account created. For the others, the card holds the name you type and the chat’s first message (the character’s greeting); you can add a description yourself.
      • Pictures. The export has no character pictures. Cards get a plain lettered picture, or one you choose.
      • Who sent each message. Chatbound lays the turns out the way a Character.AI chat runs (the character’s greeting, then you, then the character) and shows you a preview; where it goes out of step, you swap it from that message on.
      • Personality and scenario. Character.AI has no such fields, so they stay empty. Pinned memories aren’t in the export either.

      Where each app puts it

      • SillyTavern imports the cards and the chat files.
      • Janitor AI imports a card when you choose it as the character’s image. It has no way to import chat history.
      • Perchance AI Character Chat imports the cards and the chat files.
      • Sakura.fm imports the cards, not chats.

      How to check that nothing is uploaded

      Your chats are private, so this page is built to have no way of sending them anywhere, and you can check that yourself.

      1. Open this page, then turn off Wi-Fi or mobile data. Open your export: it still works, because nothing needs the internet.
      2. Watch the counter at the top. It counts every network request the page makes after you open a file (the anonymous visit count included) and stays at 0 while you read.
      3. Open your browser’s developer tools, Network tab, and open your export. No request appears while it opens, and none when you read, search or save.

      The page also carries a security policy that allows two connections and nothing else: Gumroad’s licence check, used only when you paste a Pro key, and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits to the page without cookies. In the Network tab you will see that visit count when the page loads. Your file, your email address and your chats are never sent to either, or anywhere else. The privacy page lists exactly what the page sends and stores.

      Questions

      What is the password for the Character.AI export ZIP?

      The email address of your Character.AI account, all in lowercase. The export email says so. Chatbound asks for it after you drop the ZIP and forgives capitals and stray spaces.

      Why doesn’t it show who said what?

      The export doesn’t record it. Each message in message.json has its text, a time and the chat it belongs to, and nothing about the sender. Chatbound shows the messages in time order and never invents names.

      My export email hasn’t arrived.

      Character.AI says exports can take 3 to 4 weeks. Check your spam folder and the inbox of the email on your account. If the link in the email has expired (it works for 7 days), ask for a new export in your settings. There are more fixes on the export not working page.

      Will a very large export open?

      Yes. The file is read in pieces in a background worker, so the page doesn’t freeze. In our test, a 106 MB password-protected ZIP holding 288 MB of messages (382,860 of them) opened in 9.5 to 16 seconds on a laptop across our test runs, and the page never paused for more than a quarter of a second. On a phone, very large exports work but take longer and need free memory.

      Does it work on a phone?

      Yes, in a current browser on Android or iPhone. Download the ZIP from the email first, then choose it from your Files or Downloads.

      Can I move my characters and chats to another app?

      Yes. The Move to another app tab turns the export into character cards (Character Card V2, as PNG or JSON) and SillyTavern chat files. SillyTavern and Perchance import both. Janitor AI and Sakura.fm import the cards but have no way to import old chats. Step-by-step guides: SillyTavern, Janitor AI, Perchance and Sakura.fm.

      Will the moved chat know who said what?

      Only as well as the layout you check. The export has no sender field, so Chatbound starts from how a Character.AI chat runs: the character’s greeting, then you, then the character, in turns. The preview shows the names it will write; if a chat goes out of step (for example after you regenerated replies), press “Swap from here” on the first wrong message. The text of every message is copied exactly.
