The RFM formula

Men: RFM = 64 − 20 × (height ÷ waist)
Women: RFM = 76 − 20 × (height ÷ waist)

Only the ratio of height to waist counts, so any unit works as long as both are in the same one. The women’s formula is the men’s plus 12, which is how the paper writes it: 64 − 20 × height ÷ waist + 12 × sex, with sex 0 for men and 1 for women.

A worked example: a man 175 cm tall with an 85 cm waist gets 64 − 20 × 175 ÷ 85 = 22.8%.

How to measure

  1. Height: no shoes, heels against a wall, looking straight ahead.
  2. Find the top of your hip bone at your side, and put the tape around your waist level with it.
  3. Keep the tape level and snug on bare skin, breathe out normally, and read it at the end of the breath.

The study used this landmark, the upper edge of the hip bone. Measured higher, at the narrowest waist, the waist reads smaller and RFM comes out lower.

How well RFM does

In the validation group, RFM misclassified obesity, as measured by DXA, in 12.7% of women and 9.4% of men. BMI got it wrong in 56.5% of women and 13.0% of men in the same people. It also flagged diabetes slightly better than BMI did.

The authors list the limits plainly: adults aged 20 and over only, three ethnic groups (Mexican-American, European-American and African-American), a lower accuracy in older and in leaner people, and one country. Treat the number as an estimate within a few points.

RFM, the Navy method and BMI

RFM needs two numbers and one tape. The US Navy method adds the neck, and the hips for women. BMI needs a scale, and it does not estimate body fat at all. The waist-to-height ratio uses the same two numbers as RFM and has official bands, which RFM does not.

Questions

Short answers, with the source behind each one in the list below.

How accurate is relative fat mass?

In the 2018 validation group it estimated body fat within the DXA-defined obesity line far more often than BMI: obesity was misclassified in 12.7% of women and 9.4% of men, against 56.5% and 13.0% with BMI. For one person, treat it as an estimate within a few percentage points.

What is a normal RFM?

RFM has no official bands. The study defined obesity, for its comparison only, as a DXA body fat of 33.9% or more for women and 22.8% or more for men, cut-points the authors call arbitrary, and it did not propose RFM cut-points of its own.

Why does RFM ask for my sex?

At the same height and waist, women carry about 12 percentage points more body fat, so the women’s formula adds 12. Women: 76 − 20 × height ÷ waist. Men: 64 − 20 × height ÷ waist.

Can I calculate body fat without weighing myself?

Yes. RFM uses only your height and your waist. Weight is optional on this page and only splits the result into fat and lean mass.

Does RFM work for teenagers?

No. The study included adults aged 20 and over, and the authors say it was not tested in children. Children and teenagers are assessed with measures made for their age.
