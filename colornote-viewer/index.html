    Get the backup from your phone to the computer

    ColorNote saves backups on the phone itself. Send one to yourself, then open it here. These are the steps from ColorNote's own help page.

    On the Android phone

    1. In ColorNote, open the menu and tap Backup, then the Device tab (or go to Settings, Backup).
    2. Tap Backup notes and enter your master password if ColorNote asks for it.
    3. Tap the backup file you just made and send it to yourself: by email, Google Drive or another app.
    4. On the computer, download the file (it ends in .backup) and open it here.

    The password

    ColorNote encrypts every backup. Without a master password it uses 0000, which Notelift tries first. With one, type the master password you had when the backup was made. ColorNote's automatic backups open with 0000 unless "Secured auto backup" was on.

    Moving to an iPhone

    ColorNote has no iPhone app. Open the backup here and save the iPhone pack: Notes on a Mac imports it, and iCloud brings the notes to your iPhone. All the steps, with and without a Mac.

    Questions

    Is there a ColorNote app for PC or Windows?

    Not from ColorNote. ColorNote's help page says it supports only Android and suggests running the Android app in the BlueStacks emulator. To read your notes on a computer without an emulator, open a backup here.

    How do I open a ColorNote backup file on a PC?

    Make a backup in ColorNote (Settings, Backup, Backup notes), send the file to yourself, then choose it on this page. Notelift tries ColorNote's default password, 0000, and asks for yours if the backup has one. Your notes appear in a list you can search and filter.

    Why can't other programs open ColorNote notes?

    The backup is encrypted with your backup password, and ColorNote says its notes are not readable in other programs. Notelift opens the backup with the password you give it, in your browser, the way ColorNote itself does when it restores.

    Is my backup or my password uploaded?

    No. The file is read and decrypted by this page on your own device; nothing in it, and not the password, is stored or sent. The only request Pro makes is the license check with Gumroad, which sends the license key and nothing else.

    What about notes I locked in ColorNote?

    They are listed with their title and dates, but their text stays encrypted with your lock and cannot be shown. Take the lock off in ColorNote, make a new backup and open that one.

    Can I move my ColorNote notes to an iPhone or to Google Keep?

    To an iPhone, yes: Pro saves a pack that Notes on a Mac imports, and iCloud brings it to the phone (steps). Google Keep cannot import files, so the Google pack is for Google Docs and Drive.

    Does Notelift change my backup or put notes back into ColorNote?

    It never changes the file; it only reads it. To bring notes back into ColorNote on a phone, use ColorNote's own restore. The restore list shows which notes are in your archive and trash.
